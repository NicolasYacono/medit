# Breathing Meditation - Script V4 - English (Male Voice)

[Intro]
[warm] Hello, it's a pleasure to welcome you to this meditation.

[pause 2s]

[Preparation and posture]
[calm] Find a relatively quiet place 
and take a moment to adjust your posture.

Prioritize comfort. Whether on the floor or in a chair, 
align your trunk gently, keep your head straight 
and let your hands rest comfortably on your thighs.

[pause 2s]

[gentle] Remember that it's natural for the mind to wander; 
whenever it does, with firmness and patience, 
simply bring it back.

[pause 3s]

[Phase 1: The breath]
[serene] We begin by bringing attention to the breath... 
just as it is, without changing it, without forcing it.

[pause 2s]

[slow] Breathe slowly, noticing the air flowing in and out.

[pause 3s]

[whisper] There's nothing to control. 
Simply observe the natural rhythm your body already knows.

[pause 2s]

[calm] Imagine that each inhalation and each exhalation... 
are like a wave that comes and goes, without effort.

[pause 4s]

[Phase 2: Observing the body]
[grounded] Now, slowly shift your attention to the body.

Begin at the crown of your head and gradually descend: 
the forehead, the eyes, the cheeks, the jaw.

[pause 3s]

[gentle] Without judgment, simply observe where you feel tension 
and where you feel relaxation.

[pause 2s]

Continue moving down: the neck, the shoulders, the arms, the hands.

[pause 3s]

The chest, the abdomen, the back, the pelvis, the legs, the feet.

[pause 4s]

[Phase 3: Observation without attachment]
[serene] Here, in this space, there is nothing to change. 
We simply observe, like a witnessing consciousness.

[pause 2s]

[calm] If thoughts, emotions, or sensations arise, 
we watch them pass like clouds in the sky.

[pause 3s]

[whisper] They are not you, they simply pass through your awareness.

[pause 4s]

[Phase 4: Integration]
[warm] Gradually, we begin to return to a broader awareness.

[pause 2s]

Bring your attention back to the breath, 
to the gentle movement of your body as you breathe.

[pause 3s]

[gentle] At your own pace, slowly open your eyes.

Take a moment to feel the effects of this practice.

[pause 2s]

[Closing]
[blessing] Namaste. I thank you for the time you've dedicated to yourself today.

May the benefits of this meditation accompany you for the rest of your day.

[pause 2s]
